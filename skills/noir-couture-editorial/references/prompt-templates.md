# Prompt templates

Choose one blueprint for its composition. Fill the fields using the brief and the [garment-to-type matrix](./style-dna.md#garment-to-typography-design-matrix). The optional lines below each blueprint can be added individually. Render only the quoted copy the user wants. Use the requested aspect ratio in place of the default when supplied.

## A1 · Architectural or sculptural poster

```text
Vertical 3:4 editorial studio poster. Full-length {SUBJECT} wearing {GARMENT} and {FOOTWEAR}. Frame the entire silhouette with floor contact visible and space to the left of the shoulders. Matte charcoal concrete (#141517), soft high side light across the seams and folds, a fine rim along the silhouette, natural skin and fine print grain.
Upper-left title "{TITLE_ZH}" in warm ivory, drawn with {DISPLAY_TYPE_STYLE}. Set "{SUBTITLE_EN}" beneath it in {ENGLISH_FONT_STYLE}. Place one angled crimson (#B81D24) brush underline below the title. Keep it clear of the garment.
```

For a numbered look, place “VOL. {VOL_NUM}” near the hem in the left margin. For a craft-focused poster, use one short material or construction note beside the garment detail it describes.

## A2 · Ink, lace or fluid silk poster

```text
Vertical 3:4 couture poster of {SUBJECT} in {GARMENT} with {FOOTWEAR}. Full figure on a matte ink-charcoal studio floor (#151618). Use a soft directional key and rim light to separate layered fabric at the hem and sleeves. Keep the natural skin texture and a quiet margin alongside the silhouette.
Stack the title "{TITLE_ZH}" in the upper-left margin in warm ivory {DISPLAY_TYPE_STYLE}. Place "{SUBTITLE_EN}" below in {ENGLISH_FONT_STYLE}. Echo the fabric movement with a tapered crimson (#B81D24) stroke in the surrounding negative space.
```

If the brief calls for a seal, set “{SEAL_ZH}” in a small vermilion stamp. A supplied poetic line can run down the opposite margin. Reserve clear space between vertical type and the train.

## B1 · Cover with a left-hand cover line (menswear showcase)

```text
Vertical 3:4 studio magazine cover. A seated three-quarter portrait of {SUBJECT} wearing {GARMENT}, against warm charcoal-taupe (#423E3B). Use a soft directional key to show the fabric surface and a fine rim light to separate the head from the background. Leave space to the left of the portrait and below the shoulder on the right.
Set "{MASTHEAD}" across the top in condensed high-contrast ivory lettering. The crown overlaps a small part of the masthead while the name stays recognizable. Place "{HERO_ZH}" with "{HERO_EN}" at lower right in {DISPLAY_TYPE_STYLE} and {ENGLISH_FONT_STYLE} respectively. Use a short crimson (#B81D24) dash as a divider.
```

For a second story, place “{LEFT_HEAD}” in the left margin. If the user supplies a season or department, tuck it beneath the right end of the masthead.

## B2 · Cover with a lower-left lead (womenswear showcase)

```text
Vertical 3:4 portrait-led magazine cover. Compose {SUBJECT} wearing {GARMENT} against smooth warm taupe (#504A46). Frame a seated three-quarter portrait with space at lower left. Model the face and fabric with directional studio light, natural skin texture and a fine edge highlight.
Place the large ivory masthead "{MASTHEAD}" across the top, partly masked by the crown or headpiece. Keep the word readable. Set "{HERO_ZH}" above "{HERO_EN}" in the lower-left corner in {DISPLAY_TYPE_STYLE} and {ENGLISH_FONT_STYLE}. Separate the two lines with one fine crimson (#B81D24) dash.
```

For an issue cover, align “VOL. {VOL_NUM}” under the left side of the masthead. A short collection label can sit at upper right if it clears the face and headpiece.
